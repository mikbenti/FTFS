Global Quality Flow

From market expectation to operational execution, arrival feedback and system improvement.

Mandate of Global Quality

Quality must be a technical governance function, not only an inspection function.
Global Quality converts market expectations into measurable standards and governs their execution across origins, growers and packhouses.Its purpose is to protect brand value, reduce variability and enable better commercial decisions.
Core Responsibilities
Define standardsGovern complianceAnticipate risksLead claims
  • Protect the customer promise.
  • Reduce quality variability across origins.
  • Benchmark growers, packhouses and varieties.
  • Control product release and commercial restrictions.
  • Close the loop through root-cause analysis and corrective actions.

Three Layers of Quality

The product result depends on process quality. Process quality depends on system quality.

Organization Model

Product–Origin–Market–Customer Matrix

Claims Management & Root Cause Analysis

A claim closed only with a credit note is a lost learning opportunity.
RCA Flow
  • Claim received
  • Lot identified
  • Evidence collected
  • Root cause analysis
  • Corrective action defined
  • Responsible owner assigned
  • Follow-up completed
  • Standard / protocol updated

Quality as a Commercial Value Enabler

Feedback to Genetics & Variety Strategy
